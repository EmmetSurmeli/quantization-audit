# Claims and evidence

All entries below refer to measured outputs. Positive Brier differences mean transferred minus refitted, so positive favors refitting. Brier uses the mean sum of squared multiclass probability errors, with range 0–2.

| Defensible statement | Exact evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Refitting made little practical difference in the primary setup. | `results.json: primary`: Qwen3-4B / ARC-Challenge / 4-bit, calibration n=400, test n=1,172. Transfer 0.2251724, refit 0.2234232, difference 0.0017493; 95% interval [-0.0038965, 0.0082821], 2,000 paired two-sample bootstrap replicates with refitting. | Interval entirely inside prespecified ±0.01 operational margin. Does not establish universal equivalence or a meaningful positive improvement. |
| Quantization can change answers even when calibration transfers adequately. | `prediction-changes.csv`, primary / ARC-Challenge / 4-bit: 88 changed answers among 1,172 questions; 19 beneficial, 51 harmful, 18 wrong-to-other-wrong. Accuracy 0.8771331 to 0.8498294 in `metrics.csv`. | Temperature scaling preserves answer choices and cannot recover the 2.73-point accuracy loss. |
| Four-bit weights substantially reduce serialized storage. | `resources.csv`, primary: 8,056,445,038 BF16 bytes versus 2,274,515,269 4-bit bytes, a 71.77% reduction including serialization metadata. | Not a total-system-memory, energy, cost, or speed claim. |
| Compression was not a speedup in this measured run. | `resources.csv`, primary / ARC-Challenge: mean synchronized scoring latency 0.118654 seconds BF16 versus 0.229807 seconds 4-bit per question, n=1,172 each. | Apple M5 host, sequential runs; loading, tokenization and warm-up excluded. Run order, other activity and thermal conditions are not controlled. |
| Smaller calibration pools introduce selection sensitivity. | `sample-size.csv`, primary / ARC-Easy / 4-bit / refitted: at n=50 the ten-subset Brier range is approximately 0.1052–0.1311, versus 0.1059 for n=400. | Overlapping nested subsets share a test set; ranges are not confidence intervals. No universal “50 labels suffice” claim. |
| The primary result is not an across-the-board repair effect. | `metrics.csv`: Qwen3-4B / ARC-Easy / 4-bit transfer Brier approximately 0.1030 versus refit 0.1059, difference -0.0028919. Secondary 4-bit differences range from -0.0028919 to +0.0026067 across model/task cells. | Exploratory comparisons without a family of formal significance claims. Related tasks and one model family limit generality. |
| Option formatting remains relevant. | `robustness.csv`, primary / Challenge / 4-bit, 200 paired questions: accuracy 0.865 original, 0.845 permuted; transfer-minus-refit Brier benefit 0.0071390 original versus 0.0008441 permuted. | Descriptive paired subset, not 400 independent questions; no permutation significance claim. |
| The full experiment is reproducible from cached outputs. | `acceptance-checks.json`: 472 checks passed, 30,888 records, 2,670,786 token evaluations. `reanalysis-verification.json` records exact numerical equality after restoring the weight-free archive. | Restored analysis validates recorded data and manifests; it does not rerun model inference or prove absence of benchmark contamination. |
